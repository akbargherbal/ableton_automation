# Course Outline
## Ableton Live 12 Fundamentals for AI Music Creators
**Duration:** 20 Hours (Lecture + Hands-on Labs)

> **Scope note (added in the mastering-course phased plan, Phase 5):** this outline
> documents the **click-automation UI-grounding course** (see `README.md`) — it is
> still live and authoritative *for that course*. It is **not** the curriculum for
> the separate Suno-mastering course. If you're working on the mastering course, use
> `docs/suno-mastering-course-breakdown.md` and `docs/suno-mastering-curriculum.md`
> instead — see `SUNO_MASTERING_AGENT_POLICY.md`'s "Curriculum" section.

### Target Audience
* No music theory background required.
* First experience with a Digital Audio Workstation (DAW).
* Hobbyists creating music using AI platforms (e.g., Suno).
* Learners who want to edit, arrange, enhance, and personalize AI-generated music inside Ableton Live 12.
### Learning Outcomes
By the end of this course, learners will be able to:
* Navigate Ableton Live 12 confidently.
* Import and organize AI-generated music.
* Edit audio clips and MIDI.
* Build simple arrangements.
* Use instruments and effects.
* Mix and polish a complete track.
* Export music for streaming and social media.

# Suggested Final Project
**"From AI to Your Own Production"**
Starting with a Suno-generated track, learners will:
* Import the audio into Ableton Live 12.
* Edit and restructure the song.
* Replace or layer selected parts with Ableton instruments.
* Add drums, bass, or ambient elements.
* Apply EQ, compression, reverb, and delay.
* Balance the mix.
* Export a polished final track ready for sharing.
---
### Design Philosophy
This outline intentionally avoids traditional music production topics (music theory, synthesis, advanced sound design, mastering, or recording techniques). Instead, it focuses on **editing, enhancing, and personalizing AI-generated music**, making Ableton Live approachable as a creative editing environment rather than a professional studio tool. This progression mirrors the journey of users who start with generative AI (like Suno) and want greater creative control over the music they produce.
